I make small, carefully built web apps — for my family, my friends, and the games we play.

Washington, DC. Most of what I build is used by a handful of people every day, and all of it is listed below.

GitHub

Games

  • Around Table Round

    A home for the board games my friends and I play: online rooms, computer opponents, and games that can run over days.

    aroundtableround.com

  • Commonwealth

    A cooperative civilization game of my own design, for the browser and the big screen.

    commonwealth.aroundtableround.com

  • Crossword

    A fresh themed crossword every day. Solve it alone, race your friends, or fill it in together.

    crossword.aroundtableround.com

  • Couch Blocks

    A falling-block game for up to four people sharing one well.

    couchblocks.aroundtableround.com

  • Rumble Seat Games

    Games for the car. One phone in a passenger's hands, everyone else plays by voice, and the driver just drives.

    rumbleseatgames.com

    fulltablegames.com

  • Shelfie

Everyday tools

  • Cinema Roll

    Rate movies on the things you care about, keep a record of what you've watched, and see your own patterns.

    cinemaroll.org

  • Meal Hat

    Meal planning and the grocery list that comes out of it, built for a household.

    mealhat.com

  • Every Street

    Walk every street around your house. Each day it plans a loop from your front door through streets you haven't walked.

    everystreet.app

  • We’ve Met

    Tell your phone who you just met. Weeks later, ask it "who's the woman with the whippet?" and get her name back.

    wevemet.org

  • Peace Out

    A private way out of a first date. Nothing happens unless you both want to leave.

    letspeaceout.com

  • iCorder

    Every sensor in your iPhone, on one screen. A native SwiftUI app.

    icorder.aroundtableround.com
